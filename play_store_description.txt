Why Choose Smart Expense Manager?

Unlike traditional expense trackers that require tedious manual entry, Smart Expense Manager uses AI to automatically capture and categorize your transactions from bank SMS. This means zero data entry - just install, grant SMS permissions, and let the app do all the work!

Our intelligent categorization engine learns from your spending patterns and accurately assigns transactions to the right categories. You can review, adjust, and customize categories to match your budgeting style.

With beautiful charts and insights, you'll always know where your money is going. Set budgets, receive alerts, and make smarter financial decisions with AI-powered recommendations.

Privacy First:
We take your privacy seriously. SMS message text is processed and stored on your device and is not sold or used for advertising. When you choose online AI Insights, parsed financial details such as date, amount, merchant, category, transaction type, and bank are securely sent to our service to generate personalized insights. You have control over whether to grant SMS access and can use manual expense entry without it.

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED:

SMS Permissions:
Used to read recent and incoming SMS, identify bank and payment transaction messages, and automatically track expenses. Unrelated messages are not added to expense records, and full SMS message text is not sent to our service.

Internet Permission:
Used for sign-in and optional online AI Insights. When AI Insights is used, parsed financial details—not full SMS message text—are securely sent to our service to generate insights.
