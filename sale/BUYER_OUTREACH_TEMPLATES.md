# Buyer outreach templates

## Short marketplace teaser

Exclusive acquisition opportunity: a native Kotlin Android expense-management
app that converts financial SMS messages into categorized transactions,
budgets, trends, merchant analytics, and optional AI insights. Includes source
code, parsing rules, documentation, Play listing assets, and release automation.
Best suited to a fintech, accounting, payroll, expense-management, or mobile-app
operator seeking a faster route to market. Production approval and verified
metrics will be supplied before listing. NDA and buyer qualification required
for technical access.

[Seller name]
[Business email]

## Buyer qualification reply

Thank you for your interest. Before confidential disclosure, please provide:

1. Legal buyer/company name and website
2. Your role and authority in the acquisition
3. Intended use of the app and assets
4. Indicative acquisition budget and funding source
5. Preferred closing timeline
6. Technical contact for Android/backend diligence

After qualification and a mutual NDA, I can provide verified metrics, a product
demonstration, architecture materials, the asset schedule, and controlled
read-only source access at the appropriate diligence stage.
